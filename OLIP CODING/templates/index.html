A sample 3-hour walk

  1. Meet your guide

    We start at your hotel or an agreed meeting point.

  2. Jalan Braga

    Spot art deco details and hear the stories of old cafes and shops.

  3. Coffee break

    Sit down, taste something local, and ask all your questions.

  4. Jalan Asia-Afrika

    Stand outside Gedung Merdeka and learn about the 1955 conference.

  5. Alun-alun Bandung

    Finish at the city square with time for photos.
